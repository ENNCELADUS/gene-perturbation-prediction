Preprocessing

  1. Screen. A screen is the set of pairs that one study tested in one line with one setup. An anchor study gives one screen for each anchor gene. Labels stay inside their screen. They are not merged across screens or studies.
  2. Label. The authors' call. If the study has no call, the authors' cut. If it has no cut, FDR < 0.05. Each study's rule is a row of configs/benchmarks/sl_label_rules.csv. Negatives are pairs that the screen tested and did not call. They are not true negatives.
  3. Pair. The two genes in alphabetical order, with one row for each screen and pair. Same-gene rows and control guides are removed.
  4. Unlabelled. A negative is unlabelled when the study flags its gene as depleted alone (Harle 2025, Chan 2025). No other filter removes pairs.
  5. Conflicts. A pair can be SL in one screen and not SL in another screen. Both rows are kept.
  6. Condition. Each rule row has a condition. The main result uses only genetic screens (loss of function in the parental line). Screens with a drug or irradiation (treatment), an activating anchor (gain) or a call copied from a group of lines (group) are scored and kept in the run files.
  7. One class. A screen with no positives or no negatives does not enter the metric.

Metric

Recall in the top 10%: rank the pairs of a screen, and count the known positives in the top 10% (a random ranking gives 0.10). Take the mean over the screens of a study, then over the studies of a line, then over the lines. Thus a large screen or a study with many screens does not control a line. The main result is the model minus pair identity for each line, with a bootstrap interval over lines. Validation selects the model, and test is scored once.

Lines without a ModelID

A line needs only basal scRNA and study labels to be in the benchmark. It does not need measured GeneEffect. Measured GeneEffect supervises only stage one. Stage one predicts GeneEffect from the basal scRNA of the line, and stage two reads that prediction and the study labels. Thus a line without a DepMap ModelID is in the benchmark. Its ID is its Cellosaurus accession, or SL-<study>-<line> when Cellosaurus has no record of it. The reviewed table configs/benchmarks/sl_line_identities.csv records the spellings, the ID, the parent, the patient group and the lineage of each line. Each DepMap join continues to use the ModelID. A line with basal scRNA and without GeneEffect is an unlabelled training line of the final split, so it never adds a training target to stage one. A derived line stays on the side of its parent and of the other lines from the same donor. The stage-two control with measured GeneEffect can run only on lines with a ModelID. The non-cancer lines are outside the cancer lines that train stage one, so the results show them as a separate group.

Stage one and stage two are separate. The stage-one benchmark keeps the final split. Stage two reads stage one's production table: one fit of the chosen configuration on every labelled line, with no validation set. If an SL validation or test line without GeneEffect shares a patient with labelled lines, that fit leaves those lines out and records them. The SL split draws every line with basal scRNA and labels, with or without GeneEffect, and the split table gives each line's group. hTERT-RPE1 is placed on the training side by hand (decision 2026-11-04), with its patient group PT-JWRsco. The placement is one reviewed row of configs/benchmarks/sl_split_placements.csv; the split records it under placed and in its inputs. The draw of every other line does not change.

The table gives the identity of the 15 lines. We read each accession and each parent from Cellosaurus on 2026-11-04. The patient group is the DepMap PatientID of a related line with a ModelID. If no related line has a ModelID, the group is the Cellosaurus accession of the line or of its parent. "Proposed" marks a lineage that Cellosaurus does not give directly. "No entry" tells that Cellosaurus has no record of the line; its ID is then SL-<study>-<line>.

The production fit of stage one leaves out HAP1, because it shares a patient with eHAP, an SL test line without GeneEffect.
  • Limits: stage two trains mostly on in-sample stage-one inputs, so the number for the lines without GeneEffect is a low estimate. That group is small and mostly non-cancer.
  • hTERT-RPE1, eHAP, HMLE, 293T, HeLa S3 and WA09 have prepared basal scRNA (wave 3 of the extra sources) and get a score. HEK293-A does not use the HEK-293 cells; it stays a separate line without a score.
  • eHAP iCas9 merges into eHAP and uses its basal cells, as RPE1-iCas9 merges into hTERT-RPE1 (decision 2026-11-04).

Perturb-seq

A genetic Perturb-seq screen puts a pool of guides into the cells and reads each cell with scRNA. 19 benchmark lines have such a screen. All 19 get a score.

The pipeline reads only the non-targeting cells or the cutting-control cells, as basal scRNA. It does not use the perturbed cells.
