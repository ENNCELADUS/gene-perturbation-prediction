Build

.venv-tx1/bin/python -W ignore -m src.data.builders.build_sl_study_benchmark \
  --rules configs/benchmarks/sl_label_rules.csv --raw data \
  --gene-effect data/sl_dependency_v0/raw/depmap/CRISPRGeneEffect.csv \
  --hgnc data/sl_screens/raw/hgnc/hgnc_complete_set.txt --out data/sl_studies/benchmark
.venv/bin/python -m src.data.builders.build_sl_split \
  --final configs/benchmarks/cell_line_geneeffect_split.json \
  --lists data/sl_studies/benchmark/lists.csv \
  --model data/sl_dependency_v0/raw/depmap/Model.csv --out configs/benchmarks/cell_line_sl_split.json
  • Both builders refuse an existing output. The benchmark build takes about 10 minutes on one core.
  • Raw result files: data/sl_studies/raw/<study_id>/ on the H20, listed with SHA-256 in configs/benchmarks/sl_study_tables.csv. Studies: sl_studies.csv; lines: sl_study_lines.csv.
  • Lines join only by DepMap ModelID, from the reviewed lines field of each rule row.
  • Not done: a minimum of positives per list. 13 lines with a ModelID have fewer than 10 positives.
