Stage one: GeneEffect in the SL held-out lines

The stage-one metrics of SL protocol §11. A residual is GeneEffect minus the training gene mean, divided by the gene's residual SD. Per-line residual Spearman: correlation over the selective genes in each line, mean over lines (the selector). Per-gene residual Spearman: correlation over lines for each gene. Residual skill score: within-gene R². Calibration slope: measured residual on predicted (1 is ideal). Top-20 recall: share of a line's 20 strongest dependencies in the predicted top 20. Whole-line Pearson: a sanity check that the gene mean alone passes.
