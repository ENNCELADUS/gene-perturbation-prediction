The task

The model sees a cell line only through its basal single-cell transcriptome. A single-cell foundation model reads this profile. The model never gets GeneEffect, perturbation response or SL labels of a held-out line as input.

Stage one predicts DepMap (Chronos) GeneEffect for each gene in a held-out line. Stage two ranks candidate SL partners B for a gene A that the line lost. Its ground truth is experimental SL-screen labels in named lines.

A held-out DepMap line is a model of a new tumour. The tumour has a basal single-cell profile, but it has no CRISPR screen.

Stage one: GeneEffect prediction

The metric is the per-line residual Spearman. For each held-out line, subtract the mean of each gene from the predicted and the measured GeneEffect. Then calculate the Spearman correlation over genes, and average it over lines. The metric shows if a model ranks genes by what is specific to the line. Within-gene R² measures the same thing across lines, for each gene.

The trained rows read deltas from the frozen PIE: ΔE (expression), ΔH (hidden state) and ΔL (Cellosaurus text). Each trained row has a main run and five folds on the 981 frame lines.

After the GPU changes on 2026-10-23, the stage-one rows were run again. The changes moved the results only at the rounding level. The records here still show the first runs.

Reading

  • No trained row is better than the linear prior. All contrasts with the prior are below zero on the two splits (Table 3).
  • ΔE alone has real line signal. It is better than ΔL, its line-permuted and no-Δ controls, and own expression only.
  • ΔE adds nothing to the prior. FiLM on the prior and ΔE is equal to the same model on line-permuted ΔE.
  • PIE's warm start makes the direct readout worse, by 0.07–0.09.
  • The trained rows overfit. The ΔE rows get a training selective Spearman of 0.35–0.40, but only 0.13–0.16 on validation.

Cell-state telemetry

Frozen cell-state vocabularies are fit on the 377 labelled training lines and read through the frozen PIE. The results tell which states the state-mixture model will precompute. No model trains on GeneEffect here.

The gap is what a per-state reading through PIE adds to a reading of the line's mean input. The gap is relative to the across-line spread of the whole-line delta, over 200 pilot genes.

Reading

  • The gap is small. For ΔE, it is at most 0.019 of the across-line spread. PIE reads a mix of states almost the same as the mean input.
  • Real memberships are only a little above permuted ones: 0.0004–0.005 above each permuted seed.
  • The QC states give a gap as large as any biological vocabulary. Thus a large gap alone does not show biology.
  • The deposit is the largest structure. The absolute scVI states show the line itself, not a state in the line.

Record: cell-state telemetry (code f5fefbe, 2026-10-22).

Stage two: SL partner ranking

Two heads are fit on the 29 screened single-cell training lines. They rank held-out pairs from three combinatorial screens: Ito 2021, Klingbeil 2024 and Harle 2025.

The PARIS head reads PARIS evidence and the GeneEffect of the two genes in the line. The viability-embedding head reads the stage-one embedding V of the two genes. Each context-ablated control gives all lines the frame mean of each gene.

The metric is AUPRC. A random ranking gets the hit rate.

Reading

  • Pair identity is best on all benchmarks, on test and on validation. It is also best on pairs whose SL call changes between lines.
  • Predicted GeneEffect adds nothing to the gene mean. The three predicted rows are equal to the context-ablated head.
  • Measured GeneEffect adds almost nothing (+0.017; no interval excludes zero). A perfect stage one would not change this head much on this benchmark.
  • The line's V makes the viability-embedding head worse. The signal is in the per-gene V.
  • Nothing here is evidence of SL context (SL protocol §8).

Record: SL stage two (PARIS at 186c242, stage two at 9e1c251, 2026-10-23).

Next steps

The stage-one plan (docs/specs/2026-10-17-pie-stage-one-plan.md) gives this sequence:

  1. Finish stage one. Add the bulk-encoder, 377-line and protocol-mean rows for the best arm. Add a separate bulk encoder on the 343 paired lines. Then add each gene-text source to the gene query.
  2. Data ladder and state pilot. Add the cell-bank channels with per-line QC covariates. Run the frozen-state pilot of the state-mixture model on the selected vocabularies (about 55M pairs, about 50 h). Then test planted state effects.
  3. Later. Planted marginal and joint terms, and draws with fewer cells. Then fine-tuned states, and at the end, end-to-end states.

The GPU-efficiency pass is complete. CUDA-graph training steps, fused AdamW and one-pass validation scores came in on 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23. A three-epoch FiLM run went from 258 s to 114 s (hpc/README.md).
