The task

A cell line is seen only through its basal, unperturbed single-cell transcriptome, read with a single-cell foundation model. No GeneEffect, perturbation response or SL label of a held-out line is ever an input.

Stage one predicts DepMap (Chronos) GeneEffect for every gene in a held-out line. Stage two ranks candidate SL partners B for a gene A lost in that line, scored against experimental SL-screen labels in named lines. A held-out DepMap line simulates a new tumour that has a cheap single-cell profile but no CRISPR screen.

Stage one: predicting GeneEffect

The metric is the per-line residual Spearman: within each held-out line, the Spearman correlation between predicted and measured GeneEffect after each gene's training mean is removed, averaged over lines. It asks whether the model ranks a line's genes by what is special about that line. Within-gene R² asks the same across lines for each gene. Trained rows use the frozen PIE's per-line deltas: ΔE (expression), ΔH (hidden state) and ΔL (Cellosaurus text). Each trained row ran on the 981 frame lines as a main run plus five folds.

Reading

  • Nothing beats the linear prior. Every contrast with it is below zero on both splits (Table 3).
  • ΔE carries real line signal on its own. It beats ΔL, its line-permuted and no-Δ controls, and own expression only.
  • Beside the prior, ΔE adds nothing. FiLM on the prior and ΔE is indistinguishable from the same model on line-permuted ΔE.
  • PIE's warm start hurts. The direct readout falls by 0.07–0.09 when it starts from PIE's weights.
  • The trained rows overfit. ΔE rows reach training selective Spearman 0.35–0.40 against 0.13–0.16 on validation.

Cell-state telemetry

Frozen cell-state vocabularies fitted on the 377 labelled training lines and read through the frozen PIE, to choose which states the state-mixture model will precompute. The gap is what reading each state through PIE and mixing adds over reading the line's mean input, relative to the whole-line delta's spread across lines, over 200 pilot genes. No model is trained on GeneEffect here.

Reading

  • The gap is small. At most 0.019 of the across-line spread for ΔE; PIE treats a mixture of states almost like the mixed input.
  • Real memberships beat permuted ones by little. 0.0004–0.005 over every permuted seed.
  • The QC states give as large a gap as any biological vocabulary, so gap size alone does not mark biology.
  • Deposit is the largest structure, and absolute scVI states encode the line itself, not a state within it.

Record: cell-state telemetry (code f5fefbe, 2026-10-22).

Stage two: ranking SL partners

Two heads are fitted on the 29 screened single-cell training lines and rank held-out pairs from the Ito 2021, Klingbeil 2024 and Harle 2025 combinatorial screens. The PARIS head reads PARIS evidence plus both genes' GeneEffect in the line; the viability-embedding head reads stage one's embedding V of both genes. Each context-ablated control gives every line each gene's frame mean instead. The metric is AUPRC: a random ranking scores the hit rate.

Reading

  • Pair identity wins everywhere, on test and validation, and even on pairs whose SL call varies between lines.
  • Predicted GeneEffect adds nothing over the gene mean. All three predicted arms match the context-ablated head.
  • Measured GeneEffect is nearly as flat (+0.017, no interval excludes zero): even a perfect stage one would barely move this head on this benchmark.
  • The line's V hurts the viability-embedding head; per-gene V carries its signal.
  • Nothing here is SL context evidence (SL protocol §8).
